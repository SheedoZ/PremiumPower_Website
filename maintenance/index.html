Agree the scope before work begins

The assessment helps identify which checks and work are appropriate for the equipment. The agreed scope may cover:

Engine and fluids

Condition checks, fluid levels and service requirements.

Cooling and starting

Cooling system, battery condition and starting-system checks.

Controls and operation

Control-panel indications, operating conditions and reported faults.

Testing and reports

Confirm whether operational or load testing and a service report are required.

Compare the contract terms

Ask for the visit schedule, covered equipment, consumables and parts, exclusions, reporting, and emergency-response arrangements in writing. Response times and availability are confirmed for your location and agreement; submitting a form does not book a visit.

Maintenance questions

Can I enquire without knowing the model?

Yes. Share what you know about the equipment and site. The team can ask for additional details before confirming the scope.

Are parts and consumables included?

The quotation or maintenance agreement defines which parts, consumables and labour are included and which are priced separately.

Does a request guarantee an emergency visit?

No visit or response time is booked by this form. For a current fault, call the team to discuss availability, your location and the next step.

Can I request a contract for several generators?

Yes. Include the number of generators and sites if known so the team can assess the scope.
