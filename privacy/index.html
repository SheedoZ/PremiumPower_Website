Enquiry privacy

This page explains the information submitted through Premium Power's website enquiry forms.

What you provide

The form asks for your name, phone number, project location, enquiry type and message. It can include the selected generator and rating or maintenance plan, the page language, and any optional project or equipment details you choose to add.

How an enquiry is received

When you submit the form, these details are sent to Formspree, the service used to receive and store submissions and send notifications. Premium Power uses the enquiry to assess your request and contact you.

Other website services

Pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. Some pages load Google Maps or illustrative images from Unsplash. These providers receive the technical information needed to serve their content. Following phone, WhatsApp or other external links uses the selected provider's service.

Questions about your information

Contact info@premiumpower-eg.com for questions about information you have submitted, including retention, correction or deletion. This page does not specify a company-wide retention period.
