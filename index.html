Common Questions

FREQUENTLY
ASKED

How long does delivery and installation take?

Standard delivery and installation is completed within 7–15 working days depending on the generator model and site logistics. Larger projects requiring civil works may take 3–4 weeks.

Do you provide ATS panels and full turnkey installation?

Yes — we offer complete turnkey installation including site assessment, civil foundation, cabling, ATS panels, fuel piping, and commissioning. Civil works is included as a standard part of our service, not an optional extra.

What is the warranty on your generators?

We provide a standard warranty of 1 year or 1,000 operating hours covering manufacturing defects in the engine and alternator. An extended warranty with unlimited operating hours is available as an upgrade.

Are your spare parts 100% genuine OEM?

Yes — we guarantee 100% genuine OEM spare parts for Perkins, Volvo Penta, and Doosan. Every part comes with a Certificate of Origin. We do not use aftermarket or generic alternatives.

What maintenance contract options are available?

We offer two AMC tiers. Standard: 4 visits/year, 24h emergency response, 5% parts discount. Premium+: 12 visits/year, 4h emergency response, 15% parts discount, and priority scheduling. Both include genuine OEM parts and a technical report after every visit.

Can you supply soundproof canopies for residential or hospital use?

Yes — we fabricate custom soundproof canopies locally using 2mm ST.37 steel with high-density Rockwool insulation (>70 kg/m³). Our Super Silent canopy reduces noise to 65 dB at 1 meter, making it fully suitable for hospitals, hotels, and residential compounds.

Do you offer synchronization for multiple generators?

Yes — we design and install advanced synchronization panels for multi-generator setups, including load sharing, totalizing panels, and Grid-Sync for peak looping applications. We use ComAp InteliGen controllers for parallel operation.

What fuel storage solutions do you provide?

We supply custom cylindrical and rectangular steel fuel tanks from 500L to 30,000L (single and double-wall). We also install complete Schedule 40/80 fuel piping networks and automatic transfer pump systems with float switches for unattended operation.

Which areas of Egypt do you cover?

We operate nationwide from our Cairo HQ in Heliopolis, with a main warehouse in Obour City, a Delta branch in Shebin El-Kom (Menoufia), and a fabrication workshop in Giza. We actively serve Cairo, Giza, Alexandria, New Capital, North Coast, Minia Desert, and surrounding governorates.

Can you handle SCADA integration and remote monitoring?

Yes — we design and install SCADA and remote monitoring systems using GSM/Ethernet modules linked to BMS infrastructure. Features include cloud-based logging, SMS alerts, and real-time asset monitoring accessible from any device.
